Shadow Futures research archive — 9 October 2026

The current journal-oriented author manuscripts are in journal/. Their
submission guide, source notes, and computational replication package are
stored with them. The six matching PDFs are in output/pdf/research-program/
at the repository root; identical website copies are in public/papers/.
Read or download the current papers at:
https://shadow-futures.vercel.app/papers

The six DOCX files directly in this directory are the earlier short drafts,
preserved as research history. Use journal/ for the current manuscripts.
The replication/ subfolder belongs to those earlier drafts; the current
expanded methods supplement is in journal/Replication/.

In the earlier drafts, 00 is the program roadmap. Read the five companions in numerical
order, alongside the foundational Shadow Futures v5 author manuscript.
All six documents use v5's page geometry, Times New Roman typography,
double-spaced body text, title block, abstract, JEL codes, section structure,
and references. Equations and the numerical results table remain editable
native Word objects.

01: technological scale, capability requirements, and feasible entry.
02: empirical measurement framework and a reproducible synthetic benchmark.
03: counterfactual evidence, causal attribution, and limits of learning.
04: decision value, competition instruments, and legal implementation.
05: conditional corrective taxation and instrument limitations.

These are complete research drafts. Their policy conclusions depend on
explicit assumptions and additional empirical measurement. They do not
estimate an optimal tax rate or establish undercorrection for an actual
AI firm. The roadmap specifies the studies needed for that assessment.

The replication subfolder contains the methods paper's runnable benchmark,
independent arithmetic validator, recorded outputs, and optional figure.
source_links.json supplies primary source links for the bibliographies.
Internal PDF and page-image renders were used to check all 60 initial-draft
pages. Temporary rendering and browser-check files remain outside this
archive in the ignored .tmp/ directory. The published PDFs correspond to
the current journal manuscripts rather than these earlier short drafts.
