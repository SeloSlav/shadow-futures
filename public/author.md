# Martin Erlic

Canonical profile: https://shadow-futures.vercel.app/author/martin-erlic

Martin Erlic is an independent author and researcher writing about systems, economics, self-reinforcing markets, and the future.

## Book

Martin Erlic is the author of "Shadow Futures: How Markets Choose Winners—and Lose Sight of the Alternatives." The complete free reading edition was published in October 2026. It develops the comparison-budget argument for a general audience, connecting documented cases to AI, work, ownership, competition, and taxation.

- Book and chapter guide: https://shadow-futures.vercel.app/book
- Complete free book PDF: https://shadow-futures.vercel.app/book/shadow-futures.pdf
- Current edition metadata and synopsis: https://shadow-futures.vercel.app/book.md
- Amazon edition: https://www.amazon.com/dp/B0HN91X19C

## Research

Martin Erlic is the author of "Shadow Futures: Contribution Uncertainty and the Self-Reinforcing Market," first posted in December 2025 and revised in October 2026.

- Paper: https://shadow-futures.vercel.app/paper
- Original paper and six companion research drafts: https://shadow-futures.vercel.app/papers
